# RULES AND REGULATIONS

The rules and regulations enumerated below were framed for the information and guidance of the staff composing the management and all the general employees. Any infraction or violation of the rules and regulations carry a corresponding penalty ranging from a written reprimand, or suspension, up to dismissal, depending upon the seriousness and frequency of the offense.
